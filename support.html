Common questions

I paid, but the app still shows the subscription screen

On the subscription screen, tap "Restore". Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Skysteady. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Skysteady purchase.

My flight says "Forecast not ready yet"

A forecast is worked out when you open the flight on the Forecast tab with an internet connection. Weather forecasts reach about two weeks ahead, so a flight further away than that gets its forecast as soon as it comes within range.

The forecast won't load

Working out a forecast needs an internet connection for a few seconds. Open your flight while you are still on the ground, on Wi-Fi or mobile data, and tap "Refresh". Once it has loaded, it stays on your iPhone.

Does it work in the air?

Yes. Load your forecast before boarding. In the air, the card at the top of the Forecast tab keeps showing what comes next with no signal, and everything in the Calm and Learn tabs works offline.

How sure is the forecast?

It is an estimate, and the app tells you how confident it is: the closer the flight, the better the picture. In the app, open Settings, then "How the forecast is made", to see what it is built from.

I can't find my airport

Search by city, by the airport's name, or by its three-letter code. If yours is missing, write to us.

My reminder doesn't arrive

Open iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then Skysteady, and turn on "Allow Notifications". Then open the app once.

Where is my data? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore from your iPhone backup.

How do I delete everything?

In the app, open Settings, then "Delete all my data". This cannot be undone.

An estimate for passengers

Skysteady gives an estimate made from public weather forecasts. It is not an aviation weather product and must not be used to plan or fly a flight. Always follow your crew's instructions and keep your seat belt fastened when seated. Skysteady is not medical care and is not a treatment for fear of flying.
