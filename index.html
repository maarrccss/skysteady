Skysteady

See the bumps before you board

Skysteady is an iPhone app for people who would rather know what the air has in store. A turbulence forecast for your own flight in plain words, a card that tells you what comes next while you are in the air, and calm tools that work with no signal. Your flights stay on your iPhone.

Support: questions, subscriptions, your data.

Privacy Policy: what stays on your iPhone and what doesn't.

Skysteady gives an estimate for passengers, made from public weather forecasts. It is not an aviation weather product and must not be used to plan or fly a flight. It is not medical care and is not a treatment for fear of flying. Skysteady is not connected to or endorsed by NOAA, the FAA or any airline. Made by CORELABS S.L.
