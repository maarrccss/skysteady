Skysteady

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Skysteady is an iPhone app: a turbulence forecast for your own flights, with calm tools for the seat. It is made by CORELABS S.L. ("we"). This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Your flights and everything else you enter stay on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads and no trackers in the app.
  • The forecast is worked out on your iPhone, so your route is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The only things that leave your phone are the requests for public weather files and what is needed to sell and manage the subscription.

What stays on your iPhone

Your first name (if you give one), your answers to the opening questions, the flights you save (airports, date and time, and a flight number if you add one), the forecasts worked out for them, what you said each flight felt like, and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. We have no server for this information and no way to see it.

Like every app's data, what you enter is included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are yours and are handled by Apple under your Apple account.

Reminders are created on your iPhone itself. The bump meter reads your iPhone's motion sensor only while that screen is open; its readings are not saved and are not sent anywhere. The app does not ask for your location and does not read your contacts or your Apple Health data.

What leaves your iPhone, and why

  • Requests for public weather files. To work out a forecast, the app downloads parts of the Global Forecast System, a public weather forecast published by the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) and stored on Amazon Web Services. These are the same files for everyone. The app picks them by forecast date and hour, not by your route, and the request contains nothing you entered. As with any download, the computers that hold the files see the internet address (IP address) the request comes from. We do not receive these requests.
  • Apple. Apple takes your payment and manages your subscription under your Apple ID. We never see your card or your Apple ID.
  • Superwall (Superwall, Inc., United States) shows the subscription screen and tells the app whether your subscription is active. It receives a random identifier created for the app on your phone, your purchase history for Skysteady, events about the subscription screen (for example that it was shown, or that a purchase was started), and basic technical details needed to provide the service (such as your device model, iOS version, app version, language, and the region your internet connection comes from). We send it no name, no flights, no answers and nothing else you entered. It is not used to follow you across other apps or websites.

When you tap a link in the app (for example a source, or this page), it opens in a browser, and the website you visit handles that visit under its own policy.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Why we are allowed to use this information

We use purchase information to provide the subscription you bought (performance of a contract) and anonymous information about the subscription screen to understand how the app is doing (our legitimate interest). Superwall is based in the United States and processes this information on our behalf, under its data processing terms.

How long it is kept

What you enter stays on your iPhone until you delete it or delete the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple and Superwall for as long as the law and the operation of the subscription require.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete: in the app, open Settings, then "Delete all my data". Without a subscription, tap "Delete my data" at the bottom of the subscription screen. Deleting the app also removes everything.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings, under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction or deletion of the purchase information linked to your random identifier, and to object to its use. Write to us and we will help. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

Children

Skysteady is made for adults. It is not directed at children.

An estimate, not an aviation product and not medical care

Skysteady gives an estimate for passengers, made from public weather forecasts. It is not an aviation weather product and must not be used to plan or fly a flight. The real route, height and timing of your flight will differ, and the weather can change. Your crew has better information: always follow their instructions and keep your seat belt fastened when seated. Skysteady is not medical care and is not a treatment for fear of flying. Skysteady is not connected to or endorsed by NOAA, the FAA or any airline.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
